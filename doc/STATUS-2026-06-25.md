## What's new this session

- **General OCX eventsink (S33).** Adopted the MiG Alley port's `ma_eventsink.cpp` design (renamed
  `bob_*`): the game's own `BEGIN_EVENTSINK_MAP`/`ON_EVENT` maps now drive control events via RTTI
  dispatch (`bob_evt_fire(dlg, &typeid(*dlg), id, dispid)`), retiring BoB's two targeted bridges.
  Two BoB deltas: a `(LPCTSTR,short)` `bob_evt_call` overload, and `__LINE__`→**`__COUNTER__`** in the
  registrar name (BoB's unity builds concatenate `.cpp` → `__LINE__` collided; flagged back to MA).
  Verified via genuine clicks (`BOB_TRACE_OLE`): CLoad row-select and SController combo-rebind both
  fire the genuine handlers through the one path.
- **Strategic-map unit icons (S34).** The campaign map drew terrain but no icons. Root cause (measured
  with new `BOB_TRACE_ICONS`, correcting the S27 attribution): `UpdateBitmaps` calls `DrawIcons(pDC,inter)`
  per terrain block with `inter=block∩bounds`, a Windows paint-region optimization; headless there's no
  per-region paint rect, so every `inter`=(0,0,0,0) and the world-rect cull rejects every item. Fix
  (1 line, `#if BOB_LINUX`): draw icons once over the full client bounds (the game's own pre-optimization
  call, still DEADCODE) → `cull_pass 0→768`, `drawn 0→99`. Capture:
  `doc/reference/strategic-map-icons-2026-06-25.png`.
- **Campaign sim — post-load (S35–S37).** Built the `BOB_POSTLOAD_FF` ASan repro; fixed the stale
  `fly.leadflight` formation-pointer crash in `FixupAircraft` (S36); then the load-boundary
  reference-audit found the whole post-load fatal family funnels through `ConvertPtrUID` indexing
  `pItem[]` OOB on a garbage UID — honoring its own `assert(tmpUID in [1,IllegalSepID])` on Linux
  (return the null-ref it already gives for UID==0) retires the family in one place (S37). **The
  post-load campaign sim now advances the full day** (currtime 32180→62540, 1000+ cycles, no crash).
- **Campaign sim — post-mission (S38–S40).** Built the `BOB_POSTMISSION_FF` repro and drove the full
  campaign loop on `:0` (load→intercept→fly→mission-end→return→advance); gdb pinned the crash to
  `info_grndgrp::GetCruiseAt` (a post-mission ground-group SAG with garbage `type` → `Plane_Type_Translate`
  OOB). Fixed with the S37 bounds-honor pattern (S39) → advances to `SAGDecisionPreCombat`. The S40
  SAG-level funnel-skip was a **negative result** (the `type` predicate is itself unsafe across SAG
  subtypes; reverted) — the next pass needs an empirical safe corruption invariant or the type-source fix.

## Caveats / known follow-ups (banked, evidence in PORT.md)

- **Post-load fidelity:** the S37 `ConvertPtrUID` guard makes stale UIDs non-fatal by resolving them to
  NULL — a loaded raid may not perfectly re-acquire its target. The per-reference deserialise restoration
  (`FixupAircraft`/`SetTargetUIDs` gaps) is the faithful follow-up.
- **Post-mission funnel (next R4.5 entry point):** instrument `MoveAllSAGs` to dump the corrupt SAG's
  *raw* state when the S39 clamp fires → find a **safe** corruption predicate (`Status.deaded`/`movecode`,
  not a complex-field `Evaluate()`), then the funnel-skip lands; or fix the type-source (why the
  post-mission `StartUpMapWorld` rebuild leaves a corrupt ground-group SAG in the band).
- **In-flight render UAF (bonus find, S38):** a separate use-after-free on the draw thread
  (`ThreeDee::AddLensObject`/`shape::SunItemAnim` writing to an anim buffer freed by `animptr::Delete` —
  the R1.3a/R3.9 animation-lifetime family), exposed by ASan's timing during flight. Its own item.

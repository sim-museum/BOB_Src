## Fixed and verified

| item | what |
|---|---|
| **R8** | **ALT+X out of the 3D killed the game** with a bogus *"desktop resolution is TOO LOW"* fatal on a 1920×1080 desktop. `bob_enum_display_mode` treated `idx` as a plain index, but `ENUM_CURRENT_SETTINGS` is `(DWORD)-1`: as `-1` it slipped past the bounds check and asked SDL for mode `n`, one past the end. Every current-settings query failed, the header stub left the caller's `DEVMODE` **untouched**, and `TWODPREF.CPP:401` ignored the return value — so `dmPelsWidth` was uninitialised **stack garbage**. Garbage landing in 512–1023 tripped the fatal, which is why it was intermittent. |
| **R10** | **The Tacview export stopped dead at 20.48 s of EVERY sortie.** `replayframecount` resets each 512-frame block; at the first wrap the timestamp went backwards, the monotonic guard returned 0, and the caller's `goto acmi_done` skipped every remaining sample. Fixed with a wrap-lifting counter that PRESERVES S274's de-duplication (a free-running counter would have restored the 57 MB bloat). Confirmed: **20.40 s → 232.96 s**, 5,825 markers at exactly 25 Hz, bytes/second unchanged. |

## Findings worth carrying

* **BoB's modifiers are NOT MA's.** `KEYMAPS.H` carries MA's list as `//DEADCODE` **alongside** a live replacement: 3=Left Ctrl, 4=Left Shift, 5=Msg, 6=ShMsg, 7=Overlay. Same engine, same header name, different meanings — assuming they matched would mislabel every modified binding.
* **`S` is already bound** (to the deadcoded `JOYSTICKMODETOGGLE`). The PO's centre-square padlock (R5.x) wants that key, so implementing it means deciding what happens to the existing binding.

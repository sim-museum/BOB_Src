
Hurricane and Bf109 units already have automatically selected markings ("Multiskins"), but we haven't got round to this for Spits yet. ;-) 
In the meantime you can install unit specific markings manually. 

Inside this folder you will see three sub-folders with some pictures: 
	\No-roundels_54_66_74_92_222_266_610Sq  
	\Wingtip-roundels_19_41_602_603_609Sq
	\Standard-roundels
The names are hopefully self explanatory. Whilst the most common underwing markings included a large roundel, other variants existed
typically by squadron and the squadrons that used each type are listed in the folder name. If you fly for a specific squadron 
in the campaign game you may wish to install its specific markings.

Similarly to the Hurricane, dates also have an influence here. Until August 1940 the standard RAF scheme had no underwing roundel. After 
that time roundels (of varying sizes) appeared, but it took a little time in the midst of the Battle.
 
To do this, look inside one of these folders. You will see two subfolders: \imagemap and \maskmap
Highlight and COPY these folders. Then PASTE them into your \rowan software\battle of britain\ folder. You will be asked to confirm 
that you wish to overwrite.

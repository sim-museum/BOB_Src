These files change the default 109 and 110 camouflage schemes to later ones, typically seen from September onwards. 
In this period Luftwafe Fighter schemes were starting to extend the upper surfaces down the fuselage sides with a variety of mottling effects. There was 
a lot of field variation.

To install:
You will see some subfolders: \imagemap \imgmap16 and \maskmap

Highlight and COPY these folders. Then PASTE them into your \rowan software\battle of britain\ folder. You will be asked to confirm 
that you wish to overwrite. 

If you want to get rid of them, then delete these files from the relevant directories.

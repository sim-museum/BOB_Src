These files change the default (mixed) Hurricane camouflage schemes to later ones typically seen from August 1940 onwards during the "Critical Period" 
and "Blitz" phases of the battle.

All aircraft have "sky" (type S)  undersides, most with roundels applied.



To install:
You will see two subfolders: \imagemap and \maskmap

Highlight and COPY these folders. Then PASTE them into your \rowan software\battle of britain\ folder. You will be asked to confirm 
that you wish to overwrite. 

If you want to get rid of them, then delete these files from the relevant directories.
